Research Desk bundled fonts
===========================

These variable fonts include Vietnamese precomposed characters and combining
marks. They are served from the application's own /assets/ endpoint; the UI
does not request fonts from Google Fonts or another external font service.

Newsreader, by Production Type
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader
Original: Newsreader[opsz,wght].ttf
License: newsreader-OFL.txt

Noto Sans, by the Noto Project Authors
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans
Original: NotoSans[wdth,wght].ttf
License: noto-sans-OFL.txt

JetBrains Mono, by JetBrains
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono
Original: JetBrainsMono[wght].ttf
License: jetbrains-mono-OFL.txt

The complete TrueType fonts were losslessly repackaged as WOFF containers
with fontTools. No glyphs were removed. Downloaded 2026-10-19.
